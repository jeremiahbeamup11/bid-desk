Go/No-Go Brief — RFQ 003000000088942, Trash Removal Services, Prophetstown State Park (IN DNR)

Prepared by Bid Desk, Tue Nov 24, 2026. Source: IDOA Current Business Opportunities bid packet (3 files, ~18k words), read in full.

30-second verdict

Small, simple, recurring public contract, one-page price sheet. Go if you already run 6 and 8 yd front-load routes within ~40 mi of Battle Ground, IN (Tippecanoe Co.). No-go for a Lake/Porter County hauler: the drive time eats the margin.

The basics

Compliance checklist (miss one and you can be rejected)

  • Event Detail RFQ form: unit price per month for each line, extended totals, grand total. Type or use black ink
  • Landfill or incinerator name, address, and phone (required)
  • Claiming Purchasing Preferences form: answer Yes/No on all 10 questions (Indiana Business Preference is worth checking)
  • Non-Collusion Certification and Signature page: sign, date, and fill in every field
  • Write "N/A" across any section that doesn't apply. Don't leave sections blank
  • Don't add your own terms. Say "Bidding all or none" if you won't take a split award
  • Quote valid 30 days after the due date
  • Insurance certificate on request (workers' comp and liability)
  • If you're not registered yet, register at IDOA Bidder Profile (in.gov/idoa) within 5 days of notice

Risks and catches we found

  1. Date mismatch in the packet: the service description says weekly pickups "01/01/2026 – 12/31/2026," but the contract period says 2027. The question window has closed, so price it for 2027 and footnote your assumption.
  2. Fixed price for up to 4 years with renewals. Landfill tipping-fee changes can be passed through only with documentation (25 IAC 1.1-1-17). Build in fuel escalation risk.
  3. Termination for convenience on 30 days' notice. Don't buy new containers just for this contract.
